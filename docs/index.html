    Say it

    Click the right stick, wait for the beep and talk. Pause, and it's ready to paste.

  3. CtrlV

    Paste it

    Paste it into the chat box. Changed your mind? RS again cancels it.

    Send

    Check it and send it. B backs out, as it always does.

Questions

What does it actually do in the game?

Nothing. It never presses a key in the game, there's no addon, and it doesn't touch the game's files. It watches your controller for the buttons that open chat, and when you speak, it puts your words on the clipboard. You paste them with Ctrl+V and send them yourself.

Blizzard hasn't approved Speak Forever, though, and its terms forbid third-party software it hasn't authorised, so use it at your own risk.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

SmartScreen warns about apps it hasn't seen much of yet, and Speak Forever's installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The code is open on GitHub for anyone to check, and the installer is built there too.

Does my voice go anywhere?

No. Speech recognition (OpenAI's Whisper) runs on your own PC. Speak Forever only goes online to download the voice model you choose and to check for updates, which you can turn off.

Will it slow my game down?

The recommended voice model uses about 1 GB of memory, and only works while it's turning your speech into text: about a third of a second a message on a graphics card. PCs without a capable graphics card can use a smaller model that runs on the processor.

Can I paste with my controller?

Yes, with a button mapped to Ctrl+V. Speak Forever deliberately doesn't press it for you, but your controller's own software, Steam Input, or a controller that can send keyboard keys can map a spare button to it.

I play with a keyboard. Does it work for me?

Yes. Pick any key or combination as a shortcut, press it and speak, then open chat with Enter and paste with Ctrl+V. It works like Windows+H.

What do I need?

Windows 10 (version 2004) or Windows 11, 64-bit; WoW: Forever (the beta works); and a microphone, ideally a headset. It installs for your account only, so you don't need admin rights.
